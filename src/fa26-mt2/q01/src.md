Let

<div class="math-display">
$$
A=\begin{bmatrix}6&\alpha\\\\8&12\end{bmatrix}
$$
</div>

 Each part below is independent. Find the value of <span class="math-inline">\\(\alpha\\)</span> that satisfies the given condition.

<div class="assignment-parts" markdown="1">
<div class="assignment-part" markdown="1">
<div class="assignment-part-label">a)</div>
<div class="assignment-part-content" markdown="1">
<span class="badge badge-points">3 pts</span>
<span class="math-inline">\\(\det(A)=40\\)</span>.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Solution</summary>

For this <span class="math-inline">\\(2\times2\\)</span> matrix,

<div class="math-display">
$$
\det(A)=6(12)-8\alpha=72-8\alpha
$$
</div>

 Thus,

<div class="math-display">
$$
72-8\alpha=40\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{\alpha=4}
$$
</div>

</details>

</div>
</div>

<div class="assignment-part" markdown="1">
<div class="assignment-part-label">b)</div>
<div class="assignment-part-content" markdown="1">
<span class="badge badge-points">3 pts</span>
<span class="math-inline">\\(\text{rank}(A)=1\\)</span>.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Solution</summary>

Since <span class="math-inline">\\(A\\)</span> is a nonzero <span class="math-inline">\\(2\times2\\)</span> matrix, it has rank <span class="math-inline">\\(1\\)</span> exactly when its determinant is <span class="math-inline">\\(0\\)</span>. Therefore,

<div class="math-display">
$$
72-8\alpha=0\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{\alpha=9}
$$
</div>

 For this value, the second column is <span class="math-inline">\\(3/2\\)</span> times the first column.
</details>

</div>
</div>

<div class="assignment-part" markdown="1">
<div class="assignment-part-label">c)</div>
<div class="assignment-part-content" markdown="1">
<span class="badge badge-points">4 pts</span>
<span class="math-inline">\\(A^{-1}=\begin{bmatrix}3/2&amp;-1\\\\-1&amp;3/4\end{bmatrix}\\)</span>.

<details markdown="1"><summary>Solution</summary>

The inverse formula gives

<div class="math-display">
$$
A^{-1}=\frac{1}{72-8\alpha}\begin{bmatrix}12&-\alpha\\\\-8&6\end{bmatrix}
$$
</div>

 Matching the top-left entry gives

<div class="math-display">
$$
\frac{12}{72-8\alpha}=\frac32
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad72-8\alpha=8
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad\boxed{\alpha=8}
$$
</div>

 Substituting <span class="math-inline">\\(\alpha=8\\)</span> gives all four entries of the specified inverse.
</details>

</div>
</div>

</div>
